Agricultural · Environmental · Resource Economist

Michée A. Lachaud

Associate Professor, Florida A&M University · Director of Graduate Programs, CAFS · Director, CBEEA

I study how climate, technology, institutions, and human behavior shape agricultural productivity, natural-resource use, and economic welfare.

My work combines applied econometrics, optimization and numerical methods, Bayesian statistics, randomized evaluations, and behavioral experiments to answer policy-relevant questions in agriculture, climate adaptation, energy, and development.

Research agenda

Economics for a changing agricultural world

My research connects environmental and resource economics with agricultural productivity and behavioral decision-making, with a focus on questions that matter to farmers, policymakers, and communities.

01

Climate & Agricultural Economics

Weather and climate impacts, adaptation, agricultural productivity, R&D, technology adoption, markets, and trade.

02

Environmental & Resource Economics

Natural-resource use, fossil fuels and renewable energy, environmental damages, welfare, and dynamic policy trade-offs.

03

Behavioral & Experimental Economics

Risk, ambiguity, time preferences, adaptation decisions, randomized trials, and lab-in-the-field experiments.
